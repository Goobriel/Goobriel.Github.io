About Me

From a young age I loved solving puzzles and working within structure — but I also loved creativity. I learned to love math for its unchanging nature and beautiful complexity, and I found the world of filmmaking equally fascinating.

Then on December 17th, 2010, I watched Tron: Legacy — a film that captured every inch of my desire for structure, creativity, and beauty in one place. I learned that programming existed, but had no resources to pursue it until my first semester of college. Typing "Hello World" took me straight back to being that kid in complete awe.

Today I focus primarily on web development and UI/UX, where I have the most hands-on experience. But I'm genuinely drawn to the idea of embedded and software engineering as well — my brother and I share a dream of building our own mechatronics someday. He brings the creativity and art, I bring the code. Thanks for reading — make sure to check out the projects if you haven't already!
